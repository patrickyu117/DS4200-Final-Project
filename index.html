DS4200 Final Project: Life Expectancy

Introduction:

The topic of this project is life expectancy and the various factors that influence it across different countries. The goal is to explore and visualize key metrics such as GDP, education, vaccination rates, and other socioeconomic indicators to better understand their impact on life expectancy. In addition, we would like to see the difference between countries with high and low life expectancy, and how these factors vary across different regions of the world.

About the Dataset:

The dataset used in this project was found on Kaggle, where all of the data has been sourced from the Global Health Observatory (GHO) data repository of the World Health Organization (WHO), which tracks health metrics and related factors for countries worldwide. The corresponding economic data was obtained from the United Nations website.

The dataset includes data for 193 countries and contains 22 columns and 2,938 rows, containing predictive variables such as GDP, schooling, vaccination status, and more. Missing values were imputed using a Python library SimpleImputer to ensure that the data was complete and ready for analysis.

Dataset Attributes:

Attribute Description
Country Name of the country
Year Year of observation
Life expectancy Average life expectancy in years
Adult Mortality Adult mortality rates of both sexes (probability of dying between 15 and 60 years per 1000 population)

Alcohol Alcohol consumption (liters per capita)
Percentage expenditure Expenditure on health as a percentage of GDP per capita
Hepatitis B Hepatitis B immunization coverage among 1-year-olds (%)
Measles Number of reported measles cases per 1000 population
BMI Average Body Mass Index of the population
Under-five deaths Number of under-five deaths per 1000 population
Polio Polio immunization coverage among 1-year-olds (%)
Total expenditure Government expenditure on health as a percentage of GDP
Diphtheria Diphtheria immunization coverage among 1-year-olds (%)
HIV/AIDS Deaths per 1000 live births due to HIV/AIDS (0-4 years)
GDP Gross Domestic Product per capita (in USD)
Population Population of the country
Schooling Average number of years of schooling
Thinness 1-19 years Prevalence of thinness among children and adolescents aged 10-19 years (%)
Thinness 5-9 years Prevalence of thinness among children aged 5-9 years (%)
Income composition of resources Human Development Index in terms of income composition of resources (index ranging from 0 to 1)
